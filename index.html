About the practice

Twenty-nine years.
Still independent.
Now two generations.

We're a licensed and independent financial planning practice based in the suburb of Walmer, Port Elizabeth. Lindy van Jaarsveldt Financial Services cc was established by Lindy in 1997, after many years in the industry working for a corporate company — and the practice has stayed independent ever since.

To ensure our clients have continuity, Lindy's succession plan has included the services of her daughter, Layla Williams, who joined as Director & Financial Advisor. Independence means we're not selling a house-brand policy — we work out what actually protects your income, your family, and your retirement, and recommend the product that fits.
